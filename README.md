# Acesso Legal

**Connecting Central Florida’s Brazilian community to legal resources they can actually reach.**

## About the Project

Acesso Legal is a community resource navigation project designed especially for Brazilians in Central Florida seeking information in Portuguese.

Our goal is to make it easier to find and access reliable legal information, referrals, and community resources.

## What We Aim to Provide

* **Legal Resources:** Information about legal services, legal aid organizations, and referral resources.
* **Community Support:** Food assistance, essential services, and local community resources.
* **Portuguese-Language Information:** Accessible information for Brazilians navigating services in Central Florida.
* **Resource Navigation:** Organized directories that help people identify relevant services and understand where to seek assistance.

## Our Community

Acesso Legal focuses on the Orlando region and surrounding Central Florida communities.

We aim to connect people with existing organizations, services, and reliable information so they can take informed next steps.

## Brand Identity

Acesso Legal uses an established visual identity built around these colors:

* Navy Blue: `#0B3D91`
* Brazil Green: `#007A33`
* Gold Yellow: `#FFC107`
* Light Blue: `#E6F0FA`

Brand typography: Montserrat and Open Sans.

## Important Note

Acesso Legal is a resource navigation and information initiative. Information provided through the project is not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney. Service availability and eligibility may vary, and users should verify details with the relevant organizations.
